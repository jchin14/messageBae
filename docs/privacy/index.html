Privacy

Last updated 5 November 2026

What messageBae collects

Nothing. messageBae has no analytics, tracking or servers of its own, and doesn't send your data anywhere.

What stays on your Mac

  • Your settings, panel widths and badge count.
  • Your Facebook login, stored like any web browser stores it.

Deleting the app and ~/Library/Application Support/messageBae removes all of it.

Facebook

messageBae shows facebook.com/messages. Anything you do there goes to Meta and is covered by Meta's privacy policy.
